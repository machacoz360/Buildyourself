The book

What is this book about?

Before you build the life you want, build yourself.

Many people want success, money, good relationships and freedom. But not everyone prepares to become the kind of person who can carry the responsibilities that come with them.

Build Yourself Before You Build Your Life invites you to examine yourself honestly, build discipline, master yourself and take responsibility for your decisions.

It is not a book of short-lived motivational words. It is an invitation to build the habits and capability to become someone you can depend on.

Before you build your life, build yourself.From the Author’s Note

Who is this book for?

It is written for men who want to grow in character, discipline and responsibility, especially young men entering adulthood.

It may suit students, the self-employed, people going through life’s challenges, and men who want to rebuild themselves after failure.

What will you learn?

Discipline and self-control
Self-respect and emotional control
Failure, pain and rejection
Relationships and communication
Money, work and skill
Leadership, boundaries and responsibility
Health and protecting yourself
